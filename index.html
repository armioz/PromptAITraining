Module 03

What makes a good prompt?

TASK
Use action verbs: generate, write, analyze, summarize
"Write a 3-paragraph summary of…"
EXEMPLARS
Provide examples so the AI matches your expected style
"Respond like this example: Q: ... A: ..."
FORMAT
Define the end result — structure, length, style
"Return as a table with 3 columns: Issue, Cause, Action"
CONTEXT
Limit endless possibilities — give background that matters:
  • What is the user's background?
  • What does success look like?
  • What environment are they in?
"I'm a QA engineer in a medical device company…"
PERSONA
Who do you want the AI to be? (Think: someone you wish you had for this task)
"Act as a senior ISO 13485 compliance consultant…"
TONE
Provide the feeling you are going for
"Use a formal, professional tone suitable for an audit report"

"Act as a senior production engineer with 15 years of experience in injection molding manufacturing. Identify the root cause of machine downtime spikes that occur every Monday morning on Line 3. We run 3 shifts, 5 days a week; downtime increased 40% over the past 3 weeks during the 06:00–08:00 shift change. Maintenance logs are attached. Return a numbered list of the top 5 most likely causes, each with a Likelihood score (1–5) and the data needed to confirm. Format each as: Cause: [name] | Likelihood: [1-5] | Evidence to confirm: [what to check] Use direct, technical language suitable for presenting to a production team meeting."

🏢 Facility Engineer — 2nd Floor Air Quality Investigation

"Act as an Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) specialist with expertise in indoor air quality management. Investigate elevated particulate matter (PM2.5) readings on the 2nd floor production area and propose a corrective action plan. Air quality sensors triggered alerts between 08:00–11:00 AM daily this week. The 2nd floor has 8 PCB soldering stations and a screen-printing area. The HVAC system was last serviced 6 months ago. Create an investigation checklist as a table with columns: Check Item | Priority (H/M/L) | Responsible Person | Target Deadline. 🔬 Quality Engineer — Product Defect Root Cause Analysis
■ Persona ■ Task ■ Context ■ Format ■ Exemplar ■ Tone

"Act as a Six Sigma Black Belt quality engineer specializing in medical device manufacturing. Perform a root cause analysis on a sudden 8% visual defect rate increase on our catheter assembly line. Module 04

Core Techniques

01

Zero-Shot Prompting

Give the AI a task with no examples. Works best for simple, clear instructions.

"Translate this to Spanish: Hello world"
02

Few-Shot Prompting

Provide 2–5 examples before your actual request to guide the AI's style and format.

"Q: Capital of France? A: Paris\nQ: Capital of Japan? A: ___"
03

Chain-of-Thought

Ask the AI to "think step by step" to improve reasoning on complex tasks.

"Solve this problem step by step: ..."
04

Role Prompting

Assign a specific expert role to tune the AI's perspective and vocabulary.

"You are a senior cybersecurity expert..."
05

Constraint Setting

Set explicit limitations: word count, format, tone, or what to avoid.

"In under 100 words, formal tone, no jargon..."
06

Iterative Refinement

Treat prompting as a conversation. Refine based on output until you get perfection.

"Good, now make it more concise and add examples..."
Module 05

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Bad Prompt
"Write about marketing."
→
✅ Better Prompt
"Write a 300-word social media marketing strategy for a Thai tea brand targeting Gen Z audiences on Instagram."
❌ Bad Prompt
"Make this better."
→
✅ Better Prompt
"Rewrite this paragraph to be more concise, professional, and suitable for a formal business email."
❌ Bad Prompt
"Explain AI."
→
✅ Better Prompt
"Explain artificial intelligence to a 10-year-old using a simple analogy. Keep it under 150 words."
💡 Golden Rule: If a human colleague would need clarification, your AI prompt does too.
Module 06
CREATE Framework
C
Character
Define the AI's role & persona
"Act as a senior UX designer…"
R
Request
State your exact task or question
"Redesign our checkout flow…"
E
Examples
Provide sample inputs/outputs
"Like this example: [paste]"
A
Adjustments
Set tone, style & constraints
"Formal, concise, no jargon"
T
Type of Output
Specify format: list, table, code…
"Return as a 5-row table"
E
Extras
Any special rules or context
"Avoid mentioning competitors"

Module 08

Real-World Use Cases

Prompt engineering applies across every industry and role:

📊 Generate executive reports and data summaries
"Act as a senior business analyst. Summarize the Q3 sales data below into an executive report with 5 key insights, using bullet points and percentages."
📧 Draft professional emails and proposals
"Write a professional follow-up email to a client who hasn't responded in 2 weeks. Tone: polite, firm. Keep it under 150 words."
📈 Analyze market trends and competitor strategies
"Analyze the top 3 competitors of [Brand] in the Thai beverage market. Compare pricing, target audience, and marketing channels in a table."
🤝 Prepare for meetings and negotiations
"I have a vendor negotiation meeting tomorrow. Give me 5 strong opening questions and 3 tactics to lower the price by 15%."
📋 Create SOPs, policies, and documentation
"Write a step-by-step SOP for onboarding a new employee in a manufacturing company. Format: numbered steps, max 2 sentences each."
💾 Generate, review, and debug code
"Review this Python function for bugs and performance issues. Explain each problem and provide a fixed version: [paste code]"
🏗️ Architect system designs and APIs
"Design a REST API for a food delivery app. List all endpoints, HTTP methods, request/response formats, and authentication approach."
🔍 Explain complex technical concepts simply
"Explain how WebSockets work to a junior developer with no networking background. Use a simple real-world analogy and keep it under 200 words."
🧪 Write unit tests and documentation
"Write Jest unit tests for this JavaScript function. Cover: happy path, edge cases, and null inputs. Add JSDoc comments: [paste function]"
🔐 Perform security audits and code reviews
"Act as a senior security engineer. Review this login form code for OWASP vulnerabilities and list each issue with severity and fix: [paste code]"
✍️ Write blogs, scripts, and storytelling
"Write a 600-word blog post about the benefits of AI in HR. Tone: conversational, SEO-friendly. Include a hook opener and 3 subheadings."
🎬 Generate video scripts and storyboards
"Write a 60-second YouTube script for a product demo of a wireless earphone. Include hook, value proposition, CTA, and suggested B-roll shots."
🎯 Create ad copy and marketing campaigns
"Write 3 variations of Facebook ad copy for a Thai spa targeting working women aged 28–40. Each version: headline + 2-line body + CTA."
🖼️ Write prompts for AI image generation
"Write a detailed Midjourney prompt for a futuristic Bangkok cityscape at night, neon lights, cinematic style, ultra-detailed, 8K, --ar 16:9"
🎵 Songwriting, poetry, and creative fiction
"Write a 3-verse Thai pop song about missing home. Style: melancholic, poetic. Chorus should be catchy and repeatable. Include an English translation."
📖 Create personalized learning plans
"Create a 4-week Python learning plan for a complete beginner who can study 1 hour/day. Include daily topics, resources, and mini-projects."
🧩 Generate quizzes and study materials
"Create a 10-question multiple-choice quiz on the French Revolution for high school students. Include answer key and brief explanations."
💬 Role-play practice conversations
"Act as a native English speaker. Have a job interview conversation with me for a Marketing Manager role. Give feedback after each of my answers."
🔬 Explain complex scientific concepts
"Explain how CRISPR gene editing works using a simple analogy. Target audience: high school students with no biology background. Max 250 words."
📝 Summarize long texts into key points
"Summarize the following 2,000-word research paper into 5 bullet points. Highlight the main finding, methodology, and practical implications: [paste text]"
📋 Analyze NCR (Non-Conformance Reports)
"Act as a QA engineer experienced in ISO 13485. Analyze the following NCR data and identify the top 3 root causes using the 5-Why method. Categorize by severity and suggest corrective actions: [paste NCR data]"
🔍 Draft CAPA (Corrective & Preventive Action)
"Based on this ISO 13485 non-conformance finding, write a complete CAPA report. Include: root cause analysis, containment action, corrective action, preventive action, effectiveness check criteria, and target dates: [paste finding]"
📊 Summarize Audit Findings & Gaps
"Review these internal ISO 13485 audit findings and summarize: 1) Major non-conformities, 2) Minor non-conformities, 3) Observations, 4) Overall compliance risk level. Format as an executive table."
⚠️ Risk Management per ISO 14971
"Act as a medical device risk engineer. Using ISO 14971 methodology, analyze the following hazard for a [device name]. Provide: hazard situation, harm, probability, severity, risk level (P×S), and mitigation measures: [paste hazard description]"
🏭 Supplier Qualification & Evaluation
"Create a supplier qualification checklist for a critical component supplier under ISO 13485 Clause 7.4. Include: documentation requirements, on-site audit criteria, quality agreement items, and re-evaluation triggers."
💧 Water System Issue Analysis
"Act as a facility engineer. Analyze the following water system deviation (e.g. conductivity out-of-spec, pressure drop, flow rate change). Identify possible root causes, production impact, and corrective actions per GMP/ISO standards: [paste issue data]"
💨 HVAC / Air System Troubleshooting
"Act as a cleanroom HVAC engineer. Air differential pressure in Zone A dropped below spec. Analyze possible causes (filter blockage, AHU failure, door sealing), provide an investigation checklist, and recommend immediate containment actions."
⚡ Electrical System Failure Analysis
"Act as a facility electrical engineer. An unexpected power interruption occurred in production line 3. Analyze probable causes (UPS failure, breaker trip, grounding issue), list critical impact areas, and write an 8D root cause analysis report outline."
🧹 Cleaning Validation & Deviation Analysis
"Act as a GMP cleaning validation engineer. Swab test on Equipment X exceeded the residue limit. Identify contamination sources, assess patient safety risk, propose CAPA, and recommend enhanced cleaning procedure steps."
📊 Utility Performance Trend Report
"Analyze the monthly utility data below (compressed air pressure, WFI conductivity, chilled water temp, electricity consumption). Identify deviations, early-failure trends, and recommend a preventive maintenance plan: [paste data table]"
Module 09 — Bonus

The Art of Lazy Prompting 😴

Sometimes less is more. A short, minimal prompt can be surprisingly powerful — if you know exactly when to use it.

✅ When Lazy Prompting Works
  • 🧠 The AI already has context (ongoing chat, codebase visible)
  • 🐛 Simple debugging — paste the error + minimal description
  • 🔁 Minor fixes or tweaks to a previous output
  • ⚡ Quick lookups: definitions, conversions, quick facts
  • 📝 Summarizing short texts with no special format needed
✅ Lazy but effective
"Fix this bug: [paste error]"
❌ When Lazy Prompting Fails
  • 🏗️ New feature design or complex architecture
  • 🎨 Creative work needing a specific tone or style
  • 📊 Reports where format and structure matter
  • 🔐 High-stakes outputs: legal, medical, financial
  • 🌐 Tasks requiring deep domain knowledge or constraints
❌ Too lazy — vague result
"Make a login system"
💡 Golden Rule: Start lazy, then add detail only when the output disappoints. Iterate up, not down.
🚀 Lazy Prompting Cheat Sheet
Paste the error → ask to fix
"Improve this" + paste text
"Shorter. More formal."
"Explain like I'm 5"
"Give me 3 options"
"Continue from here…"
Module 10

What is an AI Agent?

An AI Agent is a system that can take actions on your behalf using AI — based on your instructions, goals, or prompts.

Think of it as a digital co-worker that follows rules, learns patterns, and helps you get work done automatically.

Core Capabilities

  • Autonomy: Operates without constant input
  • Perception: Reads data
  • Reasoning: Decides what to do based on goals
  • Action: Takes steps

Why AI Agents Matter?

  • Reduces manual work (less time checking logs/emails)
  • Increases responsiveness (catches issues faster)
  • Scales best practices across shifts and locations
